No code. Just the design, in the order you listed.
Refineries — call it done.

Improvement exists → trains spawn. Improvement gone → player spends shields to rebuild. No sliver-HP zombie. Matches how convoy routes and pens already work (count of living things, not a HP fraction).
Freight train — delete it.

Cities already build aircraft. One less unit, one less walker, no “disband for shields” tutorial. Do it when you next open the rules; nothing in fuel or convoy references uFreightTrain.
Ports as extra fuel-train sources — do not touch .

Same unit type, same walker, same silo dump. A tiny extra onTurn in a new file (or a 10-line addition to convoyGrant if you want fewer files) that says: city has iPortFacility → spawn uFuelTrain every N turns. The existing module will pick it up the moment it is activated. Parameterize the interval next to the oil/synth ones.
Engine + aircraft factory

Two separate jobs. Do not glue them into one “germanIndustry” blob if you can help it.

Aircraft factory destroyed → that city is forced onto “Never” / accumulate. That is a one-hook module: onUnitKilled / target-lost for uAircraftFactory, set city.currentProduction to nil or the accumulate item, and block canBuild aircraft until the improvement exists again. Visible, brutal, night-bomber-relevant if those factories sit under Bomber Command. Day factories in Germany matter too.
Healing → Civ2 heals in a city at a fixed drip. You can add an onTribeTurnBegin pass: if the unit is an aircraft, sitting in an airfield city, reduce unit.damage by base + bonusFromEngineFactories. Quantified as “hit points returned per turn.” Start with param.healBase = 2, param.healPerEngineFactory = 1 (or a global living-engine-HP fraction). Only while they start the turn on the pad, as you said.

Rubber / steel / aluminum / bearings → use this for cost, not healing.

Healing is local and already claimed by engine factories. These four are the classic “Schweinfurt” lever: one global multiplier on build cost of aircraft (and later flak). livingHP / startHP → if 75% alive, costs are 100/75 ≈ +33%, or use your 750/1000 → +25% table. ToTPP can change unitType.cost or intercept shield progress. Cost is viable. Players feel it when a 109 suddenly takes an extra turn.
Do not also hang healing on them. One knob per factory family.
Armaments / flak regen

Park it. Needs flakRules and delayedAction to respawn the 2cm on the pad. Fine as a later file. Armaments HP = “turns until the flak tile is occupied again.”
Urban + power — this is the Bomber Command payoff.

You want something that is true on the night map, hits every German city, and is obvious without a spreadsheet.
Best bang, still KISS:

Urban I / II / III already sit on every city. On loss of an urban unit (or its bound improvement), subtract a flat shield penalty from that city (param.urbanShieldPenalty = X per missing district). Three districts gone → that city is a husk. Bomber Command painting a city night after night has a local, readable effect. No global fraction math.
Power plants as the global / “everything runs on electricity” lever. Count living uElectricPowerPlant vs a baseline (or vs start-of-scenario snapshot). Below 75% (your old idea), apply one multiplier to all German shield output, or to fuel-train spawn intervals (stretch 2 turns to 3, etc.). That is the “workers + current” story without touching every system.

Why split them that way: urban is where BC bombs (the city itself, night map). Power is why the whole economy stutters if they keep it up. If you invert it (urban global, power local) BC still matters, but the player cannot see which raid did what.
Do not do “50% urban+power HP → 50% of all shields and all fuel.” That couples fuel (already tuned) to housing and will make you retune trains next week.
Suggested build order when you come back

Port → extra fuel trains (almost free).
Aircraft factory down → city cannot build aircraft.
Urban missing → that city’s shields drop.
Power plants → one global German output or train-interval penalty under 75%.
Four resource factories → aircraft cost multiplier.
Engine factories → pad healing.
Flak regen last.

That order is “something you can play tomorrow” first, “spreadsheet economy” last.
Nothing gets written until you say go.
